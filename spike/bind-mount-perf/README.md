# Spike #40: bind mount versus volume

Run `./run.sh <workdir>` on a Mac with Apple Container 1.5.0 and a local `fedora` image (4 CPUs, 4 GB per guest, virtiofs bind mount versus an ext4 volume). `results.txt` holds the numbers of one run; `run2` rows repeat the measurement with caches warm and are in the file too. `nodemods` is a tracked `node_modules`-style tree (40 000 files), `large` a 153 000-file repository with 3 000 C sources. Both are generated by `gen.py`.

Result: file metadata work on a bind mount is 20 to 100 times slower than on a volume (`git status` warm 1.8 s versus 0.04 s and 8 s versus 0.1 s; the first `git status` 12 s and 40 to 74 s versus under 2 s; copying 35 s and 130 s versus under 4 s and 10 s). A CPU-bound build is the same on both (about 8 to 10 s). Seeding a volume from a bind mount is a one-time copy (18 s and 68 s).
